**Short answer:** type a command's **full name** or its **alias** at the command line and press **Enter**: `OFFSET` or `O`, `TRIM` or `TR`, `DIMLINEAR` or `DLI`. Answer the prompts, choosing options by typing the capital letters shown in `[brackets]`. Press Enter on an empty line to repeat the command, and Esc to stop it. Below, the commands are grouped by job. Each row gives the **full name, alias, what it does, and whether the free CADCraft app supports it**. The last section lists the 20 to learn first.

_cadcraft.org is an independent guide, not the official site. The CADCraft column was checked against the command catalogue of CADCraft 0.8.0 (`cadcraft-cli commands`), not taken from marketing pages._

The output reads like AutoCAD's. Here is what CADCraft 0.8.0 printed for `ID 12.5,40` and `DI 0,0 30,40`:

```text
X = 12.5000     Y = 40.0000     Z = 0.0000
Distance = 50.0000,  Angle in XY Plane = 53,  Angle from XY Plane = 0
Delta X = 30.0000,  Delta Y = 40.0000,   Delta Z = 0.0000
```

So the habit of checking a drawing with `ID` and `DIST` before you trust it carries over.

## Gaps worth knowing before you switch

CADCraft is a 2D drafting app. The project's own parity documents describe 3D modelling as future work. So **UCS, EXTRUDE, PRESSPULL, SECTIONPLANE** and the rest of the 3D toolset aren't in the catalogue. Neither are PDF underlays (PDFATTACH) or attaching raster images: an `ATTACH` command exists but answers "not available yet". If a workflow depends on these, keep AutoCAD for that part. [Open-source CAD compared](/blog/open-source-cad) lists tools that do 3D.

## The 20 commands to learn first

If you're new to AutoCAD or to CADCraft, these 20 do most of the everyday 2D work. Learn the alias with the name. After a week the alias is all you'll type.

| #   | Command   | Alias | Why it's in the top 20                            |
| --- | --------- | ----- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | LINE      | L     | Most drawings start here                          |
| 2   | PLINE     | PL    | Closed outlines you can offset and hatch          |
| 3   | CIRCLE    | C     | Holes, columns, fixtures                          |
| 4   | RECTANG   | REC   | Frames, plates, rooms in two clicks               |
| 5   | OFFSET    | O     | Walls, margins, parallel edges                    |
| 6   | TRIM      | TR    | Clean up intersections                            |
| 7   | EXTEND    | EX    | Close gaps to an edge                             |
| 8   | FILLET    | F     | Rounded corners; radius 0 makes clean square ones |
| 9   | MOVE      | M     | Put things where they belong                      |
| 10  | COPY      | CO    | Repeat elements                                   |
| 11  | ROTATE    | RO    | Turn objects to angle                             |
| 12  | MIRROR    | MI    | Symmetric parts in half the time                  |
| 13  | ERASE     | E     | Delete (or select and press Delete)               |
| 14  | LAYER     | LA    | Keep walls, text and dimensions apart             |
| 15  | MATCHPROP | MA    | Fix properties by example                         |
| 16  | HATCH     | H     | Sections, materials, fills                        |
| 17  | DIMLINEAR | DLI   | Show the sizes                                    |
| 18  | MTEXT     | T     | Notes and labels                                  |
| 19  | ZOOM      | Z     | `Z` `E` gets you back to the whole drawing        |
| 20  | UNDO      | U     | Ctrl+Z, or `U` at the command line                |

All 20 work in CADCraft with the same names and aliases. You can try them in the browser at [/online](/online) without installing anything, or get the [desktop app](/download).
