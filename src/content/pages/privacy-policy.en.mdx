## About this policy

CADCraft AI operates cadcraft.org, an independent guide to the open-source CADCraft CAD software: CADCraft download and installation guides, a comparison with Autodesk AutoCAD, tutorials and a hosted copy of the CADCraft web app. This policy explains what information we process and why. Contact us at [support@cadcraft.org](mailto:support@cadcraft.org).

## Drawings in the online app

The online version of CADCraft runs in your browser. On the free plan, drawings you create or open there are **not uploaded to our servers**: auto-save keeps your most recent drawings in your browser's storage on this device, and Save and Export download files directly to your computer. Clearing your browser data removes them.

If you subscribe to **CADCraft Online Pro**, your drawings are also saved to your account in our cloud storage (Cloudflare R2) so you can open them on other devices. Only you can access them, unless you turn on a view-only share link for a drawing; anyone with that link can open a copy until you turn it off. We keep earlier versions for 30 days. Deleting a drawing deletes all its versions. If you ask us to delete your account, we delete your cloud drawings too.

## Downloads

Download buttons link to the official CADCraft releases on GitHub. When you download a file, GitHub receives your request under its own [privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Account information

You do not need an account to use the site. If you choose to create one, we store your email address, name, profile picture (if you sign in with Google) and login sessions.

## Technical and analytics data

Like most websites, our hosting provider (Cloudflare) processes IP addresses, browser and device details and request logs to deliver and secure the site. We may use privacy-friendly analytics to understand which pages are useful; these tools may use cookies or similar technologies. Our own first-party statistics record steps such as page views, download clicks and, in the online app, the type and size of files you open or save — never their contents.
